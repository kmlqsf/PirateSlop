Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production RGBA sprite atlas for a stylized realism pirate game's distant storm-cloud wall, not a background illustration. Create ONE square 2048x2048 transparent PNG arranged as an exact 4 by 4 grid of sixteen isolated cloud CLUSTERS, each centered entirely within its own equal 512px cell, at least 32px empty padding on every side. No text, labels, borders, shadows on background, horizon, sky, sea, lightning or rain. All cloud pixels must have proper alpha with soft wispy feathering into fully transparent background. Each cluster is a complete broad chunky cumulonimbus/congestus mass with a weighty irregular rounded body and 5–12 connected swollen rounded cauliflower turrets, rounded overlapping lobes and softly shaded recesses. They must look like dense billowing clouds, NOT rocks, mountains, smoke strips, mushrooms, torn flat plates or tall thin towers. Natural overall aspect between 1.1 and 1.5 times wider than tall; fill most of each cell so main body and crown are one continuous structure. Sixteen distinct silhouettes and different internal lobe arrangements, no duplicates or repetitions. Consistent orthographic low frontal viewpoint. Physically plausible soft volumetric illumination from upper left: bluish gray midtones, cool slate blue recessed shadows, gently lighter upper-left rounded bulges. The entire body retains visible rich midtone internal lighting and depth, never a black void or white outline. Use grayscale/neutral blue-gray near-monochrome lighting, readable smooth tonal shading without dramatic contrast, crisp medium-size softly rounded details. No harsh black rim, no white edge outlining, no tiny rocky gritty noise, no excessive transparency holes inside body, no horizontal layers. This is a game texture for compositing overlapping clouds into a connected storm bank, so broad connected rounded masses and internal 3D light/shadow are essential. Actual transparent alpha background.
